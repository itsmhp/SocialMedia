Falò Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2025 · Last updated August 12, 2026

Falò is a private, invite-only group chat built around short-lived "rooms." This policy explains what data Falò handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Falò does not sell your data and does not use it for targeted advertising.

Who can use Falò

Falò is intended for adults aged 18 and older in invited private circles. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data stored on your device

By default, Falò runs locally. Your handle, avatar, Circles, rooms, messages, reactions, sparks, polls, notification preferences, and Bara recaps are stored in your device or browser storage. Your acceptance of the Community Guidelines and Terms is also recorded on your device.

Raw room messages are removed from local storage when a room fades. A small Bara recap remains until you clear local activity or reset the app.

Optional cloud account

If a build is connected to our backend and you choose email sign-in, we process the data needed to run a cloud account:

  • your email address (used only to send a sign-in "magic link" and identify your account);
  • your authentication session;
  • your profile (handle and avatar); and
  • cloud room content you create or join (rooms, memberships, messages, reactions, and reports).

Authentication and hosting are provided by Supabase, which processes this data on our behalf to operate the service. Falò does not ask for your real name, contact book, precise location, or advertising identifier, and has no public follower graph or algorithmic discovery feed.

How data is used

  • To provide private-circle chat and the room lifecycle (creation, expiry, and Bara recaps).
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To operate safety features such as reporting, blocking, and content moderation.
  • To keep the service reliable and diagnose technical problems.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We share data only with service providers (such as our hosting and authentication provider) that process the minimum technical data needed to run the service, and where required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Room messages are ephemeral and are removed locally when a room fades.
  • You can clear local activity or reset the app at any time from Settings → Data. This affects your device.
  • If you have a cloud account, Settings → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account, profile, the rooms you created, and your messages. This cannot be undone.
  • Safety reports may retain a minimal snapshot of the reported content after deletion so we can review abuse and keep the service safe.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data. Ephemeral rooms reduce retention inside Falò, but they cannot prevent another member from copying or screenshotting what they can see. Please do not share information you need to keep private or permanent.

International processing

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data-protection laws.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise account deletion in the app, and contact us for other requests using the link below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Falò evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be submitted through our public support form: Falò support form. Please do not include message content, email addresses, invite links, or other private data in a public report.
