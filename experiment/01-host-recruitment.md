# 1. Recruit 8 Hosts

[⬅️ Kit](README.md) · [2. Run a room ➡️](02-create-and-vote-plans.md)

Target: **8 hosts** who have a real group of **4–12 friends**, confirmed within **2 days**.

## What makes a good host

- Has a "crew" with an **active-ish group chat** — people who like messing around online together.
- The kind of person who **starts** the jokes/threads, not just lurks.
- Willing to help test for 2 weeks (casual, not a heavy commitment) — light **at least 2 rooms**.

## Where to look

- Classmates / same cohort / same major.
- Student clubs / organizations / committees.
- Boarding-house / housemates.
- Hobby community (if that's your chosen beachhead).

## 3-minute interview (gauge "the vibe is real")

Ask the prospective host:

1. "What's your crew's group chat like — dead, or always going off?"
2. "When it's most fun, what's happening? (late-night nonsense, hot takes, gaming, gossip…)"
3. "If there were a little room that only lasts a night — be as silly as you want, then it's gone — would your crew jump in?"

> If their crew barely talks or they can't picture people being playful, they're **not** the right first host. Look for a crew that already has spark.

## Invite script (DM or group chat)

> Hey! I'm trying a little something over the next 2 weeks: tiny group chat "rooms" for a crew that **only last a night** — pile in, be silly, and when the timer's up it fades (or you all vote to keep it alive 🔥). No install, nothing serious — I'll run it in a throwaway group. Want your crew to be one of the testers? Just you + 4–12 close friends.

## Days 1–2 target

| Target | Number |
| --- | ---: |
| Hosts invited | 12–15 |
| Hosts confirmed | >= 8 |
| Total people gathered across circles | >= 60 |
